In the sidebar this page is labeled **Users**. The page title is **User Timeline** — identity-centric activity across SSH, Teleport, username sources, and related presence.

There is no Linux/macOS scope toggle. The view follows identities, not hosts.

---

## List and risk filters

| Filter | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Full identity roster |
| **At risk** | Identities with elevated risk signals |

Each row summarizes risk, recent presence, and key events. Open a user for **summary** then **timeline** detail, with host and time filters.

---

## Drill-down

From a user's timeline, jump into [Investigate](/data/investigate) scoped to that identity's activity, or into related [Anomalies](/security/anomalies).

<Note>
**Users** tracks identities. [Activity](/data/activity) tracks file actions by application. [Investigate](/data/investigate) is the raw event stream. All three connect, but they answer different questions.
</Note>
