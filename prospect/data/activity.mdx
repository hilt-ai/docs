---

Activity shows **file actions by application** — uploads, creates, deletes, and edits across the fleet. It is the application-centric view of risky file movement, distinct from [Users](/data/users) (identities) and [Investigate](/data/investigate) (raw events).

---

## Filters

| Control | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| **Applications** | Narrow to specific apps |
| **Actions** | Upload, create, delete, edit |
| **Sort** | Newest or oldest |
| **Time** | Window presets |

---

## From alert to evidence

Rows are fleet file-action alerts. Open a row for a detail drawer with host, path, actor, and timing. Use that context to continue in [Investigate](/data/investigate) or [Files](/data/files).

Operator reference: [Activity on docs.hilt.ai](https://docs.hilt.ai/dashboard/activity).
