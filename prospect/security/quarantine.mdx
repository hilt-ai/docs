---

Quarantine isolates hosts and shows blast radius when you need to stop data movement quickly. Hilt contains by **host network isolation**, not inline blocking of individual users.

---

## Active quarantines

The list shows isolated and pending hosts. Expand a host for:

- **Blast radius** — what else is in scope
- **Network mini-map** — blocked or relevant traffic
- **Readiness** — whether isolation can proceed cleanly

Fleet posture controls let you quarantine or release hosts from this page.

---

## When to use it

Pair quarantine with [Investigate](/data/investigate) and [Anomalies](/security/anomalies) so you know why a host was cut off. Exposure intervention and malware findings often lead here.

Operator reference: [Quarantine on docs.hilt.ai](https://docs.hilt.ai/dashboard/quarantine).
