Frameworks maps live detections to industry models so coverage reads as coverage — not only a list of alerts. Coverage is driven by the focused macOS / EDR host from the collector sidebar.

---

## Available frameworks

| Framework | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **MITRE ATT&CK** | Technique coverage and detections on the matrix |
| **Cyber Kill Chain** | Active chains, depth, and break points |

Hub cards summarize live stats — for example techniques detected, active chains, and deepest phase.
