Alerts is the hub for deterministic detections. Three tabs: **Alerts**, **Rules**, and **Sensitivity**. ML triage lives on [Anomalies](/security/anomalies).

---

## Tab: Alerts

The queue of rule breaks — severity, rule name, actor, host, and time. Open a row to see evidence and pivot into [Investigate](/data/investigate) or related [Anomalies](/security/anomalies).

---

## Tab: Rules

Custom **if-this-then-alert** rules over live telemetry. **New Rule** and **Create with AI** open the builder. Rules can evaluate **per event** or as a **time aggregate** (count / sum / average over a window) with conditions on process, file, network, and identity fields.

When a rule matches it raises a finding into the Alerts queue.

---

## Tab: Sensitivity

Maintain the registry of sensitive file paths so scoring and rule evaluation treat those locations with higher weight.

Operator reference: [Alerts on docs.hilt.ai](https://docs.hilt.ai/dashboard/alerts).
