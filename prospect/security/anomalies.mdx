Anomalies is the triage surface for behavioral (ML) findings. The hub has two tabs: **Anomalies** and **Anomaly Windows**. Host-type filters can narrow Linux vs macOS collectors without a hub scope toggle.

Rule-based alerts are managed under [Alerts](/security/alerts), not here.

---

## Tab: Anomalies

Switch between **Graph** and **Tree** layouts. Selecting a finding opens the investigation center with evidence chapters, session tree, and graph views.

Findings are ordered for review (risk, time, confidence, urgency). Typical actions:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | Confirm as a real anomaly |
| **Deny** | Mark benign / false positive |
| **Suppress** | Deny and suppress matching future patterns |

Use host and time filters to focus the queue.

---

## Tab: Anomaly Windows

Schedule recurring windows where **behavioral (ML) anomalies are hidden for all hosts** — useful for known-noisy maintenance or batch jobs.

---
