## Your Infrastructure, Your Data

Hilt deploys inside your cloud account. You control the environment, networking, and access. Hilt does not require standing vendor access to production systems to operate day to day.

## Metadata-Only Collection

Collectors observe metadata—who accessed what, when, and how—not file contents. Sensitive data stays in your environment while still giving security teams the visibility they need.

## Zero Trust Architecture

Traffic stays on private networking where possible. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest with customer-managed keys. The platform is designed for environments that cannot send raw telemetry to third-party SaaS backends.

## Enterprise Ready

Hilt supports compliance documentation, audit logging, role-based access, and operational controls for large teams. Customer docs cover deployment, administration, and trust & security in detail once you're onboarded.
