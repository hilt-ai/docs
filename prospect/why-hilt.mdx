Your stack can clear every access check and your data can still leave. DLP predicts what should not leave. Forensics reconstructs what already did. **Hilt watches data move** — across cloud, SaaS-adjacent workloads, AI agents, and endpoints — at runtime.

---

## The gap

| Approach | Strength | Blind spot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Predictive DLP | Policy before the act | Misses permitted-but-anomalous patterns |
| Classic EDR | Threats on the host | Not built as a data-movement control plane |
| After-the-fact forensics | Deep truth | Too late to contain |

Hilt keeps EDR for threats and adds **movement governance**: identity-resolved findings, lineage, and host quarantine when the pattern is wrong.

---

## Deployment posture

- Single-tenant in **your** cloud
- Metadata-only by default (content inspection is a deliberate choice)
- Collectors off the critical path
