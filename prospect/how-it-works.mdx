---
title: "How It Works"
description: "Collect, enrich, score, investigate, and contain — the Hilt DMG loop."
---

Hilt follows a single loop from kernel to narrative:

1. **Collect** — Linux eBPF and macOS Endpoint Security collectors ship metadata-only telemetry (process, file, network) to your single-tenant control plane.
2. **Enrich** — Identity, workload, and network context attach to every move.
3. **Score** — Behavioral models and custom rules flag anomalous patterns — not just denied actions.
4. **Investigate** — Analysts (and Hilt's guided investigation center) turn findings into identity-resolved narratives with lineage.
5. **Contain** — Quarantine hosts and intervene on third-party exposure without inline user blocking.

The product thesis: every individual move can look permitted. **The pattern is the breach.**
