Hilt is **Data Movement Governance (DMG)** — runtime visibility and control over how data moves across cloud workloads, Kubernetes, and endpoints. It deploys in your infrastructure, collects metadata-level telemetry, resolves identity, scores behavioral anomalies, and helps you investigate and contain risky movement before it becomes a breach.

Historically this space was labeled data detection and response (DDR). Hilt advances that idea: as fast as predictive tools, as accurate as forensics, at runtime.
