Account → **Profile** is your personal account page inside Settings.

---

## Account identity

Shows your **username** and **role**. A **Password last changed** line shows when you last updated your password.

Click the pencil next to your username to rename it.

---

## Change password

Provide **current password**, **new password**, and **confirmation**. Strength indicators:

| Indicator | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **8+** | At least 8 characters |
| **Aa** | Mixed case |
| **123** | At least one digit |
| **#!** | At least one special character |

After a successful change you are signed out and must sign in again.

---

## Active sessions

Lists sessions across devices (this device badge, IP / location, last activity). **Sign out everywhere** terminates all sessions.

---

## Recent sign-ins

Chronological login events (time and IP). Expand to show the full history.

---

## API keys

Create bearer keys for the [REST API](/api/overview). Each key shows name, prefix (`hilt_live_…`), scope, creation / last-used, expiry, and status.

- **Create API key** — name, **Read-only** or **Read & write**, optional expiry
- Full key shown **once** at creation
- **Revoke** invalidates immediately
- Up to 20 active keys; keys inherit your host and team restrictions
