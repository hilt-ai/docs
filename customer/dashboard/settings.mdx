The Settings page is your personal account management page. It is available to all authenticated users (hidden in static and demo modes).

---

## Account identity

The top of the page shows your **username** and **role** (Admin, Analyst, Responder, Viewer, or Owner). Below the username, a **Password last changed** line shows when you last updated your password (or "not available yet" if your password has never been changed).

### Renaming your username

Click the pencil icon next to your username to edit it. Type a new username and confirm, or press Escape to cancel.

---

## Change password

A form to update your password. You must provide your **current password**, choose a **new password**, and **confirm the new password**. The new password and confirmation must match before the form can be submitted.

### Password requirements

The password strength indicator shows four criteria:

| Indicator | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **8+** | At least 8 characters |
| **Aa** | Mixed case (uppercase and lowercase) |
| **123** | At least one digit |
| **#!** | At least one special character |

After changing your password, you will be signed out and must sign in again with the new credentials.

---

## Active sessions

A list of your currently active sessions across devices. Each session shows:

- **Device indicator**: "This device" badge for your current session.
- **IP address / location**: where the session originated (shown as "Local network" for loopback addresses).
- **Last activity**: how recently the session was active.

### Sign out everywhere

The **Sign out everywhere** button terminates all your active sessions across all devices. You will be redirected to the login page.

---

## Recent sign-ins

A chronological list of your recent login events, showing the sign-in time and IP address. The section initially displays **5 entries**. Click **Show all (N)** to expand and view the complete history.

---

## API keys

Create and manage bearer keys for the [REST API](/api/overview). The table
lists each key's name, prefix (`hilt_live_…`), scope, creation and last-used
times, expiry, and status (active, revoked, or expired).

- **Create API key** opens a dialog to name the key, choose **Read-only** or
  **Read & write** scope (write adds the ability to accept/deny findings),
  and optionally set an expiry (30 days, 90 days, or 1 year).
- The full key is revealed **once** after creation — copy it before closing
  the dialog; only a short prefix is shown afterwards.
- **Revoke** invalidates a key immediately: its next API request is rejected.
- You can hold up to 20 active keys. Keys inherit your own host and team
  restrictions — they can never access more than your account can.
