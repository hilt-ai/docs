Anomalies is the triage surface for behavioral (ML) findings. The hub has two tabs: **Anomalies** and **Anomaly Windows**.

Detection accuracy concepts (how models score and how feedback improves them) are covered here rather than on a separate page.

---

## Tab: Anomalies

Subtitle reflects how many findings need review versus total in the window.

### Presentation

Switch between **Graph** and **Tree** layouts. Selecting a finding opens the investigation center with evidence chapters, session tree, and graph views.

### Queue and verdicts

Findings are ordered for review (risk, time, confidence, urgency). Typical actions:

| Action | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Accept** | Confirm as a real anomaly |
| **Deny** | Mark benign / false positive |
| **Suppress** | Deny and suppress matching future patterns |

Use host and time filters. Host-type filters can narrow Linux vs macOS collectors without a hub scope toggle.

---

## Tab: Anomaly Windows

Page title in product: **Behavioral Anomaly Suppression**.

Schedule recurring windows where **behavioral (ML) anomalies are hidden for all hosts** — useful for known-noisy maintenance or batch jobs. This replaces the old standalone Behavioral Suppression admin page.

Rule-based alerts are managed under [Alerts](/dashboard/alerts), not here.
