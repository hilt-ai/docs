Query is the fullscreen chat assistant for your deployment. Reach it from the **top-bar Ask** control (not the sidebar). Ask about telemetry, anomalies, hosts, and how Hilt works.

Placeholder copy in product: *Ask about your data, anomalies, or platform help…*

---

## What it answers

- Live telemetry questions (processes, files, network, anomalies)
- Product how-to questions by searching the documentation shipped with your deployment

The agent chooses telemetry, docs, or both per question.

---

## Conversation UX

- Starter chips on empty state
- Thinking indicator for long-running queries
- History sidebar and new-chat control
- Hand-off from the header Ask bar while already on `/query`
