Alerts is the hub for deterministic detections. Three tabs: **Alerts**, **Rules**, and **Sensitivity**.

---

## Tab: Alerts

The queue of rule breaks — severity, rule name, actor, host, and time. Open a row to see evidence and pivot into [Investigate](/dashboard/investigate) or related [Anomalies](/dashboard/anomalies).

---

## Tab: Rules

Subtitle: *Custom if-this-then-alert rules over live telemetry*.

### Rule list

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **On** | Enable / disable |
| **Name** | Rule name and label |
| **Severity** | High / medium / low |
| **Fires** | Fire count |
| **Last** | Last fire time |
| **Actions** | View related findings, edit, delete |

**New Rule** and **Create with AI** open the builder. Rules can evaluate **per event** or as a **time aggregate** (count / sum / average over a window) with conditions on process, file, network, and identity fields.

When a rule matches it raises a finding into this queue (and can surface alongside ML findings depending on configuration).

---

## Tab: Sensitivity

Subtitle: *File paths treated as sensitive across risk scoring and alerts*.

Maintain the registry of sensitive paths so scoring and rule evaluation treat those locations with higher weight.
