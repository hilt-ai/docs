Quarantine isolates hosts and shows blast radius when you need to stop data movement quickly.

Subtitle reflects how many hosts are currently isolated.

---

## Active quarantines

The list shows isolated and pending hosts. Expand a host for:

- **Blast radius** — what else is in scope
- **Network mini-map** — blocked or relevant traffic
- **Readiness** — whether isolation can proceed cleanly

Fleet posture controls let you quarantine or release hosts from this page.

---

## How isolation works

Hilt contains by **host network isolation**, not inline blocking of individual users. Pair quarantine with [Investigate](/dashboard/investigate) and [Anomalies](/dashboard/anomalies) so you know why a host was cut off.
