Exposure tracks third-party dependencies: what you granted versus how they actually behave.

Two hub tabs: **Landscape** and **Intervention**.

---

## Tab: Landscape

Subtitle: *Third-party dependencies: the grant vs the behavior*.

Filters commonly include **All / Needs attention / Blocked / In baseline**.

Each dependency shows drift between expected scope and observed behavior (erratic or out-of-policy access). Open a dependency for scopes, evidence, and actions such as blocking.

Evidence is endpoint-flavored (Endpoint Security / Network Extension). There is no Linux/macOS hub toggle on this page.

---

## Tab: Intervention

Subtitle: *Autonomous response policy, per dependency*.

Configure how Hilt should respond when a dependency drifts — per-dependency policy board for automated or guided intervention.
