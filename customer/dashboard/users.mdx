In the sidebar this page is labeled **Users**. The page title is **User Timeline** — *Track user activity across your infrastructure*.

There is no Linux/macOS scope toggle. The view is identity-centric (SSH, Teleport, username sources, and related presence).

---

## List views

| Filter | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **All** | Full identity roster |
| **At risk** | Identities with elevated risk signals |

Each row summarizes risk, recent presence, and key events. Open a user for **summary** then **timeline** detail, with host and time filters.

---

## Drill-down

From a user's timeline, jump into [Investigate](/dashboard/investigate) scoped to that identity's activity, or into related [Anomalies](/dashboard/anomalies).

---

## Not this page

Sidebar **Activity** is a different surface: [file actions by application](/dashboard/activity). Do not confuse Users (identities) with Activity (file-upload detection).
