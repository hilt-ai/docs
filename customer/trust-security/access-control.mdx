Access to a Hilt deployment is governed at two layers: your users authenticate to the dashboard and are constrained by roles and teams, and Hilt engineers reach your environment only through your approval workflow. Both layers are auditable.

---

## Dashboard authentication

The dashboard authenticates against accounts stored in the deployment's dashboard database (PostgreSQL-backed authentication). There is no email invitation flow; an administrator creates each account directly with a username and a temporary password (minimum 8 characters), which the user replaces after first sign-in. Disabling an account blocks sign-in reversibly; deleting an account removes it and all its sessions.

For identity provider integration options, contact your Hilt representative. See [User Management](/administration/user-management) for account lifecycle detail.

---

## Roles and permissions

Each user holds one role. Roles map to fixed permission sets:

| Role | What it can do |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Owner** | Same as admin; fixed on the seeded account and cannot be assigned, changed, or deleted |
| **Admin** | Full access, including managing users, teams, and the audit log |
| **Analyst** | Full data access and detection rule management, no admin pages |
| **Responder** | Like analyst, but cannot create, edit, or delete detection rules |
| **Viewer** | Read-only access plus the AI query page |

Sensitive actions are gated by specific permissions, including *manage users*, *manage teams*, *manage alerts*, *manage rules*, and *quarantine hosts*. See [User Management](/administration/user-management) for the full role reference.

## Team scoping

Teams constrain which hosts a user can see. A team owns a set of hosts, its members see those hosts, and a child team is limited to a subset of its parent's hosts.

- A **global admin** (an admin or owner with no team) sees everything.
- A **scoped admin** is an admin assigned to one or more teams and manages only that subtree.
- Team membership, set on the user record, is what controls host visibility.

Team-based scoping requires PostgreSQL-backed authentication. See [Teams](/administration/teams).

## Audit logging

Security-relevant actions are recorded to the audit log, which administrators manage. Logged events include:

- Quarantine and release actions, with the user who initiated them and the timestamp (see [Quarantine](/dashboard/quarantine))
- Anonymized export events, with the export ID, row count, date range, and approval status (see [Anonymized data export](/on-prem/data-export-overview))
- User account changes; after a user is deleted, audit history retains entries under the username

Because the audit log lives in the dashboard database in your account, the record stays under your control.

---

## Hilt engineer access

Hilt engineers do not hold standing access to your environment.

- **Approval-gated.** Access is granted only through your approval workflow, established during provisioning.
- **No standing admin.** There is no persistent Hilt administrator account in your deployment.
- **Logged.** Access granted through your workflow is recorded by your cloud account's own logging.
- **Scoped and time-bound.** Access is intended to be limited to the task and revoked afterward. The exact approval mechanism and time bounds are defined in your agreement and provisioning runbook.

Because the deployment runs in your account, you can revoke Hilt access at any time using your cloud provider's IAM controls.

<Note>
For sensitive operations such as host quarantine, the action runs through your dashboard under a permissioned user and is recorded in the audit log. Quarantine requires the deployment to hold the IAM permissions to modify security groups in your account, granted during provisioning. See [Quarantine](/dashboard/quarantine).
</Note>
