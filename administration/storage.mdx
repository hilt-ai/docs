The Storage page shows how much telemetry data your deployment is storing, broken down by hot (fast-access) and cold (archival) tiers. This page requires the **view audit** permission.

---

## Summary

The top of the page shows three key metrics:

- **Total storage used** — combined hot and cold tier usage.
- **Hot tier** — data stored in the fast-access layer (ClickHouse), used for real-time queries and dashboards.
- **Cold tier** — data archived to object storage (S3) for long-term retention at lower cost.

---

## Time range

A time range selector lets you view storage trends over different windows: **7d**, **30d**, **90d**, or **1y**.

---

## Tier explorer

An interactive breakdown of storage by dataset (e.g., process events, network flows, file mutations). Each dataset shows its hot and cold tier usage, compression ratio, and retention policy. Click a dataset to view or edit its lifecycle policy.

---

## Hot / Cold comparison

A chart comparing hot vs. cold storage usage over time. Helps you visualize how data flows from the hot tier to cold storage as it ages past the configured retention window.

---

## Cold storage policy

Shows the current cold storage configuration:

- **Retention period** — how long data is kept in cold storage before deletion.
- **Compression** — the compression algorithm used for archived data.
- **Storage class** — the S3 storage class (e.g., Standard, Glacier).

---

## Pricing estimate

An estimated monthly storage cost based on current usage and configured storage classes. Provides a breakdown by tier so you can evaluate cost optimization opportunities.

---

## Insights

Automated recommendations based on your storage patterns — for example, datasets with low query frequency that could benefit from shorter hot-tier retention, or unusually high growth rates worth investigating.

---

## Connectivity

A live indicator shows whether the storage health endpoint is reachable. If the connection drops or data becomes stale, a banner appears with a retry option.
