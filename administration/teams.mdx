Teams organize your hosts into a scoped hierarchy and control which hosts each user can see. A team owns a set of hosts; its members can see those hosts, and child teams are limited to a subset of their parent's hosts. This page requires the **manage teams** permission and is available only with PostgreSQL-backed authentication (hidden in static admin mode).

## Page layout

The header has a **Teams** title and a **New Team** button. A filter bar searches across team names, host IDs, and member usernames.

Teams render as an expandable tree (up to three levels deep). A **global admin** also sees a **Global Users** section at the top — users who belong to no team (plus the owner and the seeded admin) — which can be expanded to list them. A scoped admin sees only their subtree.

When there are no teams yet, the page reads **"No teams yet. Create one to start assigning hosts."**

---

## Team cards

Each team in the tree shows:

- An expand/collapse control (when it has sub-teams).
- The team **name**.
- A badge with its host count, and sub-team count when applicable.
- An **Admins** row (the members whose role is admin or owner), and a member count.

If you can manage the team, a row of action icons appears: **Show hosts & members**, **Add sub-team**, **Edit hosts**, **Rename team**, and **Delete team**.

### Detail panel

**Show hosts & members** expands a two-column panel:

- **Hosts** — each assigned host with a status dot, its host ID, and host-type badge.
- **Members** — the team's members, each with their dashboard role badge.

Note that there are no separate per-team roles — a member's badge reflects their global dashboard role, and a team's "admins" are simply the members who are admins or the owner.

---

## Creating a team

**New Team** (or **Add sub-team** on an existing team) opens a drawer:

- **Team name**.
- **Parent team** — **Top level (Global)** for a global admin, or a specific parent. A top-level team may include any host; a child team is limited to its parent's hosts.
- **Hosts** — once a parent is chosen, a host picker lets you select which hosts the team covers (search by name, ID, or type).

---

## Assigning members

Members aren't added on this page — a user's team membership is set on the **User Management** page (when creating or editing a user). Team membership is what controls which hosts that user can see.

---

## Host scoping and visibility

A few rules govern how host visibility works:

- A team's assigned **hosts** define which collectors its members can see.
- A child team's hosts must be a **subset** of its parent's hosts. The host editor enforces this, and you can't remove a host from a parent while a child team still uses it.
- Editing a team's hosts is done through **Edit hosts** (the host picker), then **Save hosts**.

---

## Deleting a team

**Delete team** asks for confirmation, warning that members will lose access to that team's hosts and that the action can't be undone. A team that still has sub-teams can't be deleted — remove or move its sub-teams first.
