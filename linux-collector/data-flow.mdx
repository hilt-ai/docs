---
title: "End-to-end data flow"
description: "How events travel from the collector to the dashboard."
---

Events generated by the collector follow a simple pipeline from the host to the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collector">
    Produces events and sends them in batches to the control plane.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ingest service">
    The endpoint resolves via PrivateLink to an internal NLB fronting an ECS service in your VPC. Each event is published to Amazon MSK (Kafka) for buffering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrichment layer">
    An EKS deployment consumes the MSK topic, enriches events, and writes normalized data into ClickHouse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Classification and drift detection">
    Events are scored, and scheduled jobs evaluate model drift against historical data in ClickHouse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dashboard">
    Accessible via internal ALB.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For full infrastructure details, see the DMG Control Plane Documentation by Hilt.
