The Settings page is your personal account management page. It is available to all authenticated users (hidden in static and demo modes).

---

## Account identity

The top of the page shows your **username** and **role** (Admin, Analyst, Responder, Viewer, or Owner).

### Renaming your username

Click the pencil icon next to your username to edit it. Type a new username and confirm, or press Escape to cancel.

---

## Change password

A form to update your password. You must provide your current password and choose a new one.

### Password requirements

The password strength indicator shows four criteria:

| Indicator | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **8+** | At least 8 characters |
| **Aa** | Mixed case (uppercase and lowercase) |
| **123** | At least one digit |
| **#!** | At least one special character |

After changing your password, you will be signed out and must sign in again with the new credentials.

---

## Active sessions

A list of your currently active sessions across devices. Each session shows:

- **Device indicator**: "This device" badge for your current session.
- **IP address / location**: where the session originated (shown as "Local network" for loopback addresses).
- **Last activity**: how recently the session was active.

### Sign out everywhere

The **Sign out everywhere** button terminates all your active sessions across all devices. You will be redirected to the login page.

---

## Recent sign-ins

A chronological list of your recent login events, showing the sign-in time and IP address. Click **Show all** to view the complete history.
