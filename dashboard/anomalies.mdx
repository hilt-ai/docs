The triage queue for behavioral findings. Each finding represents a deviation detected by the anomaly scoring system (or raised by a detection rule); this page is where you review them and mark each one as a real anomaly or a false positive.

## Page layout

The page is a split view: a **finding queue** on the left and a **detail panel** on the right. On smaller screens you see one at a time, with a **Back to findings** button to return from a finding to the queue.

The header shows how much is waiting (e.g. *"3 to review · altogether 18 total"*) and carries the shared controls:

- **Filters** popover (left).
- **Host selector** (*All hosts* by default) and **time presets** — **5m / 15m / 1h / 6h / 24h / 7d**.

The list refreshes automatically every 30 seconds.

---

## Filters

There's no free-text search; filtering is structured through the **Filters** popover:

| Section | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | All · Activity spike · Network · Sensitive file · Lateral movement · Detection rule |
| **Severity** | All · Critical · High · Medium · Low |
| **Source** | All · Detection rules · ML anomalies |
| **Sort by** | Time · Severity · Confidence · Urgency |
| **Visibility** | A **Suppressed** toggle to reveal findings hidden by prior feedback |

Active filters appear as removable chips beneath the header. When you arrive scoped to a detection rule, a **Rule: {name}** chip shows the active scope.

---

## Finding queue

The queue is split into three tabs by review state:

| Tab | Contents | Empty state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Review** | Findings awaiting a verdict | "All caught up — No findings need your attention." |
| **Accepted** | Findings you confirmed as real | "No accepted findings." |
| **Denied** | Findings you dismissed | "No denied findings." |

If nothing matches the current filters at all, the queue shows **"No findings — No anomalous behavior was detected in this time window."**

### Finding cards

Each finding is a card showing:

- **Headline** — the finding's narrative headline (or its behavior label).
- **Relative time** and short **hostname**.
- **Type** — e.g. *Type: Activity spike* (when classified).
- **Verdict** — an **Accepted** or **Denied** badge once reviewed.
- A one-line **summary** of what happened, with inline event chips.
- A **cluster** badge when the behavior is atypical for the host.
- **User / Reviewer** and **Confidence** (High / Medium / Low).
- A **Host quarantined · View** link when the host has been isolated.

The selected card is highlighted with a green accent. Severity is available as a filter but isn't printed on the card.

## Finding detail

Selecting a finding opens its report in the right panel. (If nothing is selected, the panel prompts you to *"Select a finding from the queue to view details."*) The report has two parts.

### Narrative

The top band explains the finding:

- **Headline**, with the **host** and finding **time**.
- A **type** badge and a **confidence** badge (hover for "Model Confidence").
- A **Quarantined** link when applicable.
- The narrative itself — *what happened* and a **Why unusual** section — plus context chips.
- **Identity** badges for the job, application, or workload involved.

Two context banners may appear: a **workload phase change** note (explaining that volume-only signals were de-emphasized), and a **feedback-suppression** note when the pattern matches a prior finding a reviewer denied, including the pattern similarity (an exact match or a percentage).

### Reviewing a finding (Confirm / Dismiss)

In the narrative band you act on the finding:

- **Confirm** — mark it as a real anomaly (moves it to **Accepted**).
- **Dismiss** — mark it as benign or a false positive (moves it to **Denied**).
- A notes field (*"Add review notes..."*) lets you record context.

After you give a verdict on the Review tab, the queue automatically advances to the next item. Dismissing a finding for a quarantined host can release the host — you'll see a *"released from quarantine"* confirmation.

### Event timeline

Below the narrative, a **swimlane timeline** plots the evidence across a five-minute window in three lanes — **Process**, **Network**, and **File**. Nodes are colored by how strongly they contributed (the legend covers *key contributor*, *involved*, *background*, and *strongest link*), and clicking one pins a tooltip with the PID, parent, time, a relevance score and rank, and its linked events. Connecting arcs are labeled *strong*, *moderate*, or *weak link*. If a finding has no trace evidence, the panel notes that no trace data is available.

---

## Refresh behavior

The findings list and the open finding's detail both refetch every 30 seconds. The queue isn't paginated — it's a single scrollable column for the current filters.
