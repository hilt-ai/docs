Groups your hosts by how they behave and shows how those groups shift over time. Hosts with similar activity form **clusters** (behavior groups); as behavior changes between periodic **refits**, hosts move between clusters. This page visualizes both the current grouping and the movement.

- **Header** — title, the subtitle _"How hosts group and shift over time"_, and a `{N} clusters · {M} hosts` summary.
- **Insight strip** — a card summarizing the most notable change (e.g. _"3 hosts moved into Network-heavy"_), with a top driver metric and an **Investigate Event** button when relevant.
- **Canvas** (left) — the fleet visualization, with a refit timeline beneath it.
- **Evidence rail** (right) — context panel that changes with your selection.

---

## Fleet canvas

The canvas plots the current snapshot:

- **Cluster bubbles** — one per behavior group, sized by host count and colored per cluster, with the label and host count shown. Large bubbles may include a short narrative. Emptied-but-referenced clusters appear as dimmed "ghost" bubbles.
- **Idle** and **Returned** pools — special groupings (bottom corners) for hosts with insufficient telemetry and hosts that have reappeared.
- **Movement arrows** — curved flows between clusters, with width scaled to the number of hosts and labeled `{N} hosts`.
- **Host dots** — individual hosts inside their bubble (when the Hosts layer is on).

### Layers

A small toolbar (top-left of the canvas) toggles overlays. Clusters are always shown; you can turn **Movements** and **Hosts** on or off. Movements are unavailable on the very first refit (there's no previous snapshot to compare). On very large fleets, enabling Hosts focuses you into a cluster (host dots are only drawn within a selected cluster).

### Navigating

- **Scroll** to zoom and **drag** to pan; a **Reset view** control re-centers.
- **Click a cluster bubble** to open its detail (click again to deselect).
- **Click a movement arrow** to inspect that flow.
- **Click a host dot** to open that host's trajectory.
- **Click the Idle or Returned pool** for its detail panel.
- **Click the background** to clear selections.

---

## Refit timeline

Beneath the canvas (when there's more than one refit), a **Timeline** control scrubs through snapshots over time. Drag or click the track to jump to a refit, or use **Play** to replay the snapshots in sequence. Changing the refit clears the current selection.

A refit is a periodic re-clustering of the fleet — each point on the timeline is one snapshot of how hosts were grouped at that time. (This control moves through time; it does not change clustering parameters.)

## Evidence rail

The right rail shows different content depending on what's selected.

### Activity summary (default)

With nothing selected, the rail shows **Activity summary** — the recent shifts (up to three flows, e.g. _"_{N} _hosts →_ {cluster}_"_ with a driver) and the main **behavior groups** with their host counts. If nothing moved since the previous refit, it says so.

### Cluster detail

Selecting a cluster shows its label and host count, an **Investigate Event** button, an optional narrative, a **Cluster Profile** (metric bars grouped into Network, Process, and File), and the member **Hosts** list. The host list can be filtered to **Moved** hosts (which carry a _from_ {cluster} badge) and paginates after the first 10.

### Movement detail

Selecting a flow shows {N} **hosts moved**, the _from → to_ clusters, the refit time range, and a confidence level (high/medium/low). **Behavior Shifts** summarizes the typical change across the moved hosts with signed percentage bars, and an expandable **Hosts** list breaks down each host's deltas.

### Host trajectory

Selecting a host (or arriving with a host deep-link) shows its **current cluster**, an optional narrative, its **recent trajectory** across the last few refits as a color strip, and its **most recent move** with the top metric deltas that drove it.

### Pool detail

Selecting the **Idle** or **Returned** pool explains what the pool means and lists its hosts (returned hosts show the cluster they rejoined).
